How to play

Each round is one puzzle: a 5x5 grid of tiles valued 1 to 6, and two goals that must both hold at the same moment. Look at the board, then bid how many turns you need (1 to 12). Once the bid is locked you cannot change it, and there is no undo.

Actions, one per turn

  • Slide a row or column one step. The tile pushed off the edge wraps around to the other end.
  • Swap two tiles that touch sideways or up and down.
  • Nudge a tile up or down by 1. A 6 nudged up becomes 1, and a 1 nudged down becomes 6.
  • Draw a random replacement for one tile from the bag. The tile you toss goes into the bag afterwards, so it cannot come straight back on the same turn. Draws are the only random action.

Rows are numbered 1 to 5 from the top and columns 1 to 5 from the left. Tap a goal to highlight its cells. An ascending run reads left to right, top to bottom, and along a diagonal from its top end. A full house is three of one value and two of another. An any row goal holds when at least one row meets the condition.

Scoring

Every puzzle has a hidden par: the turn count of the best draw-free solution found by the solver (always 6 to 10). Par is revealed when the round ends. Using more turns than your bid is a bust and scores 0. Otherwise you score a Base set by how far your bid sits above or below par, plus a small bonus for finishing under your bid.

Base = 100 − 15 per turn bid above par (floor 10), or 100 + 25 per turn bid below par.
Bonus = 0, 5, 7, 8, 9, 9… for 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5+ turns under the bid. It never reaches 15, so one extra turn of insurance always costs more than any number of overtricks can recover.

How to play

1The idea

You get a grid of letter tiles and a fixed number of turns: a 5×5 board with 20 turns, or a 6×6 board with 25 turns (pick on the home screen). Every move costs one turn.

Rearrange the grid into words, lock the words in to bank their points, and lock as many tiles as you can before the turns run out. The score counts at the end of the board; the header always shows it as it stands.

New game is a run of levels, each with a goal score to reach. Free play is one board with no goal and one undo.

2Controls

Everything happens on the board. Each move costs one turn:

  • Swap: drag a tile onto one of its four neighbours and let go. Locked tiles cannot move.
  • Slide: flick any tile of a row or column, or drag it a long way along the line (past the edge of the board works too). The whole line moves one step and the tile pushed off the edge wraps round to the other end. Locked tiles stay put and the rest slide around them.
  • Nudge: press and hold a tile, then pick the letter before or after it in the alphabet (A and Z wrap round, so any letter is at most 13 nudges away, and the wrap can turn an A into a Z). Locked tiles can be nudged too.
  • Draw: press and hold a tile, then choose draw to replace it with a random letter from the bag. The old letter goes into the bag afterwards, so it cannot come straight back. Draw is the only random action and cannot be undone. The bag button in the header shows what is left.
  • Lock: tap the first letter of a word, then its last letter. The word locks at once and its points are banked.
  • Wild (two turns, so it needs two turns left): press and hold a locked tile and choose ★. A wild tile reads as any letter in any word but scores no points itself.

In free play, the Undo button in the header takes back your last move or lock, once per board. It cannot follow a Draw. When the last turn is spent, press Score board to finish, or undo first. Locking the last tile scores the board at once.

The ? button during a game opens a step-by-step tutorial that shows each move on a board.

3Words and locking
  • A word is 3 to 5 letters (3 to 6 on a 6×6 board) in a straight line: a row, a column, or a diagonal. It may be read in either direction, so tap the letters in the order you read them. A word may sit inside a longer run of letters.
  • Words are checked against a list of about 28,700 English words. No proper nouns or abbreviations.
  • Locking banks the word's points and marks its tiles green. Locked tiles never move again: a line still slides, but its locked tiles stay put and the other tiles slide around them, and they cannot be swapped. They can still be changed in place: nudged, drawn over, or made wild.
  • You may lock a new word that reuses locked tiles, as long as it adds at least one tile that was not locked yet. Crossing words is the main way to lock the whole board.
  • You cannot lock the same word twice, and you cannot lock both a word and its plural (DOOR and DOORS, BOX and BOXES, CITY and CITIES): pick one.
  • Nudging a locked tile may break the word it was locked for; the points already banked stay banked.
4Scoring

Word = sum of its letter values, +5 for a 4-letter word, +15 for a 5-letter word, +30 for a 6-letter word (6×6 boards).
Letter values: A E I O U L N S T R = 1 · D G = 2 · B C M P = 3 · F H V W Y = 4 · K = 5 · J X = 8 · Q Z = 10 · ★ = 0.

Final score = banked words + a coverage bonus that grows with every tile locked from the 20th (the 29th on 6×6). There are no penalties.

Since every lock costs a turn, a few words that cross each other usually beat many short ones, every tile on the ladder is worth an extra 5, and clearing the whole board early is worth a lot. Tap the score in the header at any time for the breakdown.

5Levels and the goal

New game starts at level 1. Before you play each board, a bot plays the same board behind the scenes and its word points (its coverage and spare-turn bonuses do not count) set the goal, shown under the header once it is ready. The moment your score reaches the goal the level is passed: the bar lights up, play on that board stops, and Next level takes you to a fresh board. Use up the turns (or lock the whole board) below the goal and the run ends.

The goal is a share of the bot's word points that grows with the level and never stops growing: 50% at level 1, then 5 percentage points more per level, so level 11 asks for everything the bot found and later levels ask for more. Your coverage and spare-turn bonuses count toward it, so filling the board is what carries a run past the bot. Goals are rounded up to the nearest 10. Harder boards therefore get lower goals.

Level runs have no undo, no board replay and no end-of-board summary; tap the level bar at any time for the breakdown.

6The bag and the 6×6 board

The tiles are dealt from a bag of 98 Scrabble-style letters (no blanks): 25 on a 5×5 board (73 stay in the bag), 36 on a 6×6 board (62 stay). Every tile shows its point value in the corner.

The 6×6 board gives you 25 turns instead of 20, allows words of up to six letters (+30), and its coverage ladder runs from the 29th tile to +40 for all 36. Levels and free play work the same way on both sizes, and each size keeps its own personal best.

7Options and stats

The settings (gear button) can list the words currently on the board, with their points, as a hint; tap a listed word, then Lock. It is off by default. Sounds can be switched off there too.

After a free-play board you can replay the same board (same letters, same draws). Replays do not count towards your stats or personal best, but the game remembers your best score on that board and tells you when you beat it. Your best scores and recent games are kept on this device; see Stats.
